Church Management App

Simple and organized church management for growing churches.

About the App

Church Management App is a church management platform designed to help churches organize their members and manage everyday church activities in one place.

The platform helps church administrators, pastors, ministry leaders, staff and members stay connected and organized.

Features

  • Member management
  • Family management
  • Church departments and ministries
  • Attendance management
  • Event management
  • Visitor management
  • Follow-up management
  • Church announcements
  • Notifications
  • Reports and dashboards
  • User and role management

For Churches

The platform is designed for churches that want to reduce manual work and manage church information and activities through a centralized system.

Church administrators can manage information according to their assigned permissions and church responsibilities.
