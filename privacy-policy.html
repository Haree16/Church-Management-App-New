Privacy Policy

Church Management App

1. Introduction

Welcome to Church Management App ("App", "Service", "we", "us", or "our").

Church Management App is a church management platform designed to help churches manage members, families, attendance, events, visitors, follow-ups, announcements, notifications, reports, and related church activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, protected, and disclosed when you use our App and related services.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Provide church-management functionality.
  • Manage church members and families.
  • Manage attendance.
  • Manage events.
  • Manage visitors and follow-ups.
  • Send announcements and notifications.
  • Provide reports and dashboards.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain and improve the Service.
  • Protect the security of the Service.
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized access or abuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Church-Managed Information

The Service allows churches to create and manage records about members, visitors, staff, volunteers, and other individuals associated with church activities.

Information entered by a church administrator may be provided and controlled by the relevant church.

Churches are responsible for ensuring that information they collect and manage through the Service is handled appropriately and in accordance with applicable laws and their own privacy practices.

5. Multi-Church Data and Access Control

Church Management App is designed as a multi-church SaaS platform.

Information belonging to one church is intended to be logically separated from information belonging to other churches.

Access is controlled according to the user's identity, church association, and assigned role.

Platform administrators may have broader administrative access, while church administrators and other users are intended to access only information permitted by their assigned role.

6. Notifications

If notifications are enabled, the Service may use push notification services to deliver church announcements, event reminders, attendance-related messages, follow-up reminders, and other church-related communications.

You may manage notification permissions through your device settings where supported.

7. Passwords and Authentication

We use authentication and security mechanisms designed to protect user accounts.

Passwords are not intended to be stored in readable plaintext form.

If you use the password-reset feature, a password-reset link may be sent to the email address associated with your account. Reset links may expire and may be limited to one-time use.

8. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • Authorized Church Users: Information may be accessible to authorized administrators or users within the associated church according to their permissions.
  • Service Providers: We may use service providers for hosting, databases, authentication, email delivery, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, or other services required to operate the Service.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the Service, or prevent fraud or abuse.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include encryption in transit, authentication controls, role-based access controls, secure password storage, session controls, database security controls, and monitoring.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain church records, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet contractual requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.
