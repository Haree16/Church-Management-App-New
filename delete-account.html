Information to Include

To help us process your request, please include:

What Happens After Your Request?

  1. We receive your account deletion request.
  2. We may verify the request to protect your account from unauthorized deletion.
  3. We process deletion of applicable account and personal data.
  4. Information that we are legally required or permitted to retain may be retained for the applicable period.

Some information in the Church Management App may be entered and managed by a church administrator.

If your information is maintained as part of a church's records, the relevant church administrator may also be responsible for managing or removing those records.
